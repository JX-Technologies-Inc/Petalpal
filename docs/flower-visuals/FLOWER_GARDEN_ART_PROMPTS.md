# External Garden art prompts

These are prompts only. No art has been generated. Apply FLOWER_GARDEN_ART_PRODUCTION_SPEC.md to every delivery. Attach the referenced original source as identity guidance and a Garden scene reference for camera/style only. Request three separate PNG files, not a triptych or contact sheet.

## Tulip — detailed reference template

Source reference: `Resources/Surprise & Excitement & Curiosity/Tulip.png`.

Create THREE matched, independent PetalPal Garden assets of Tulip. Preserve warm coral pink and yellow cupped tulip flowers with smooth broad tapering green leaves. Redraw the plant from a substantially overhead / light-isometric Garden camera, showing bloom tops, partially visible stems and natural foliage rooted at one logical soil contact. Soft semi-realistic painterly fantasy, elegant, restrained contrast, harmonized source colors, natural soft edges, coherent soft daylight and enough detail for zooming. The three images must share camera, canvas, lighting, rendering language, species identity and lower-middle root convention. Neutral base art only.

The old reference specifically contains a warm coral/pink Tulip and a yellow Tulip. Retain both color families across the set, recognizable smooth cup-shaped petals and broad tapering leaves. Do not replace these with roses, lilies or invented petals. Open the view enough to see into the cups without turning them into flat discs. Foliage should spread naturally around a single converging root. Keep the visual mass modest so neighboring plants can coexist. Tulip A can use one major cup plus a restrained second bud, B two to three stems sharing one base, C three to five cups/stems arranged as one richer plant. These are three natural growth arrangements, never copies of one cutout. They must appear planted when composited into PetalPal grass, with no grass painted into the PNG.

A — Sparse: airy asymmetry. B — Normal: balanced everyday cluster. C — Full: richer but still small and rooted at one point. Use approximately 1–2 major blooms/stems for sparse, 2–3 for normal and 3–5 for full where morphology supports it; botanical plausibility takes precedence.

Deliver separate 512 × 512 RGBA PNGs: `tulip_garden_sparse.png`, `tulip_garden_normal.png`, `tulip_garden_full.png`. True alpha transparency with clear margins on every edge. Use the same provisional normalized root target (0.50, 0.84); report the actual root if different for metadata review. Transparent padding, not image-center placement, accommodates species proportions.

Prohibit front-facing botanical poster composition, bouquet/sticker appearance, hard outline, white fringe, rocks, grass rectangles or patches, paths, unrelated flowers, labels, text, checkerboard, black/white/colored matte, baked background, emotional glow, sparkles, mist and VFX. Do not redesign the Garden. Do not duplicate, rotate, crop, recolor or composite the source PNG to fake a redraw.
