Delete your PetalPal account and data

PetalPal is a Jastrevia product. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Request deletion by email

Email contact@jastrevia.com from the email address associated with your PetalPal account. Ask to delete your account and associated data. Do not send your password, verification codes, tokens or Journal text. We will verify control of the account before processing the request. If you cannot access that mailbox, contact support for an identity-verification alternative.

Open deletion request email

This opens your mail app; your request is sent only when you send the email. If no mail app opens, use your webmail and write to the address above.

What deletion removes

Your profile, private Journals and attached photos, Events, derived memories and embeddings, saved reflections and evidence, Garden/Flowers, sent social messages, friendships, requests, blocks and reports submitted by or about your account are erased from the active app database. Firebase sign-in identity deletion is retried if temporarily unavailable. App-controlled local private caches are cleared when in-app deletion or sign-out completes.

What may remain

Minimal security and cost-reservation audit records and identity-deletion retry records may remain to prevent abuse, replay and incomplete erasure. Backup expiry and external processor retention depend on approved operational policies. Other users’ copies cannot be recalled. Contact support about provider-held data; no promise of immediate backup or third-party erasure is made.

Support will acknowledge the request after it reaches the staffed inbox, explain verification and expected completion timing, and confirm completion or outstanding provider work. No verified response-time guarantee is available for this draft.
