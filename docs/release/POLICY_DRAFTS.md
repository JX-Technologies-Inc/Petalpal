## 1. Privacy Policy

PetalPal helps you record feelings and experiences, grow a visual Garden, exchange social encouragement and optionally generate AI-assisted reflections. It is not a medical or emergency service.

**Information we process.** Account email, Firebase user identifier, display name, selected avatar, time zone and preferred language support sign-in and personalization. Firebase handles authentication credentials and persistent sessions. PetalPal stores private Journal text and selected attached photos, Event text/dates and chosen mood, Garden/Flower state, friend relationships/requests, visits, Flower messages, blocks and reports. Optional AI features create emotion labels, Event summaries, embeddings, reflection reports and supporting references. Optional voice input sends a recording for transcription; the transcript becomes editable text and is saved only when you save the entry. We also process consent/version records, operational security events, request identifiers, minimal AI cost/reservation metadata and deletion retry records. Hosting providers may process network identifiers and technical request metadata; confirm their logging settings before publication.

**Purposes and visibility.** We use this information to provide accounts, save your entries, render the Garden, deliver requested social interactions, enforce safety/privacy choices, investigate reports, protect the service and complete deletion. Journals and attached photos are owner-only and are excluded from Event memory and weekly/monthly reflections. Events and derived AI data are owner-scoped; social Garden responses omit private Event/Journal text. Profiles, flowers, visits and messages are visible on permitted social surfaces. Do not put sensitive information about yourself or others into social messages. A block prevents contact and Garden access in both directions; it does not erase copies already received by others.

**Processing services.** Google Firebase provides authentication. The current architecture includes Cloudflare for web delivery/Workers AI/AI Gateway, a Render backend and PostgreSQL storage; deployment location, database operator and logging retention require owner confirmation. Optional AI may send the minimum eligible Event text, selected mood, dates, derived memories and supporting Event excerpts to Cloudflare Workers AI and/or Anthropic through Cloudflare AI Gateway according to feature configuration. The in-app AI disclosure identifies configured feature paths. OpenAI support in the gateway library is not an active product feature and this draft does not authorize enabling it. No new provider may be enabled without reviewing disclosure, contracts and consent. Data may be processed outside your country; no data-residency, zero-retention or no-training guarantee is made without verified terms for the actual account settings.

**Choices.** AI is optional and off by default for new accounts. Read the processor disclosure in Settings before saving an explicit AI Processing choice. Personalization and Long-term Memory are separate dependent options. You may withdraw in Settings. Withdrawal blocks future eligible work and stale output persistence; it cannot recall data already sent. Disabling Memory deletes derived memory and saved reflection data in the active database while preserving your original Events/Journals. Typed journaling remains available without AI or microphone permission. You can report objectionable social or AI content in-app; AI reports include a reference and reason, not your full private entry. Photo and microphone access are requested only for the corresponding feature. Voice used to compose a Journal is still external transcription.

**Device storage and security.** The app uses Firebase session persistence and app-controlled storage for preferences and owner-scoped Garden placement/cache data. Browser versions may use browser storage for authentication and preferences. Sign-out and confirmed app-data deletion clear app-controlled private caches and close the session; operating-system backups, historical snapshots and issued tokens are not claimed erased. Transport uses HTTPS/WSS in approved release configuration; server authorization protects owner resources. These controls do not imply a security certification or an absolute guarantee.

**Retention and deletion.** Active entries remain until you delete them or your account, subject to the approved operational policy. In-app deletion removes the account and associated active database records. Identity-provider failures are tracked for retry without restoring erased data. Minimal security/cost reservations and deletion records remain for abuse/replay prevention and completion tracking. Their current code has no automatic expiry; [OWNER MUST SET RETENTION PERIODS AND IMPLEMENT PURGING]. Backup retention, restore exclusion, support correspondence, provider copies and log expiry require approved schedules and provider requests. See the Account & Data Deletion Policy below. We will explain applicable retention exceptions when responding to a verified request.

**Rights, children and changes.** Contact the verified support channel to request access, correction or deletion and ask about applicable rights. [OWNER/COUNSEL MUST CONFIRM PROCEDURE, MARKET-SPECIFIC RIGHTS AND CONTACT DETAILS]. Intended audience and age access rules are not finalized. Do not select a Kids/Families audience without implementing and verifying the applicable requirements. Material changes to AI processing require renewed disclosure and consent. Updated policies will identify their effective date and explain significant changes.

## 4. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In Settings, choose Delete Account, read the explanation, confirm permanent deletion and sign in again if your authentication is older than five minutes. Without the app, email contact@jastrevia.com from your account mailbox using the external deletion request page [APPROVED URL TO BE PUBLISHED]. Never email passwords, tokens, verification codes or private Journal text. Support verifies account control and communicates expected timing; lost-mailbox recovery requires an approved alternative procedure.

Deletion removes the active account, Journals/photos, Events, Garden/Flowers, messages, visits, friend links/requests, blocks, applicable reports and evidence, AI memories/embeddings and reflections/jobs. Other users' unrelated resources remain. Database erasure is transactional. Firebase identity deletion follows; if it fails, a durable retry record remains and the app reports pending identity cleanup rather than claiming complete deletion. Old identity recreation is denied. App-controlled private caches are cleared when the app receives confirmation of data erasure; an uninstalled/offline device, backups, third-party provider copies and recipients' copies cannot be erased by a database cascade.

Security/cost-reservation and deletion retry/completion records are retained under [OWNER-APPROVED RETENTION SCHEDULE REQUIRED]. No automatic purge is currently implemented for these minimal records. Backups must expire and restored backups must honor deletion records. Support must request provider erasure where applicable and explain any legitimate exception.
