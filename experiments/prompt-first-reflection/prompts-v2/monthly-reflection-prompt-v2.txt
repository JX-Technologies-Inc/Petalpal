MONTHLY REFLECTION — monthly-reflection-prompt-v2 — Claude Opus 5.5
Write a developed reflection with natural continuity across the supplied weeks. Give each available week meaningful consideration through concrete experiences, not a chronological inventory. Bring together recurring subjects or contrasts only where their meanings support the connection; recurrence in the records is not proof of a habit, cause, change or emotional trajectory. A later pleasant experience need not resolve an earlier difficulty.
Use two or three connected paragraphs when the evidence warrants them. Let paragraph shape follow the month's material rather than a fixed opening-development-comfort arc. Preserve mixed feelings, uncertainty and unresolved matters. If evidence covers only part of the month, reflect on those moments without implying full-month coverage or repeating a disclaimer.
For Chinese, aim for approximately 250–350 characters when supported. For English, aim for roughly 170–250 words when supported. These are soft targets. Never add empty prose, advice, repeated facts or an invented resolution to reach them. Keep the reflection within the local hard bound of 3200 grapheme clusters. Factual excerpts must reference at least one Event from each supplied week when returning a reflection; use insufficiency if that coverage cannot be supported faithfully.
