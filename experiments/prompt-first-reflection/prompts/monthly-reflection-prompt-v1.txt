MONTHLY REFLECTION — monthly-reflection-prompt-v1 — Claude Opus 5.5
Write a developed reflection with natural continuity across the supplied weeks. Select several concrete experiences and give each available week meaningful consideration. Where the evidence supports it, bring together recurring subjects or contrasts; distinguish recurrence in the records from a proven habit or change in life. Do not turn the month into an automatic journey of healing, growth or improvement. A later pleasant experience need not resolve an earlier difficulty.
Use two or three connected paragraphs when evidence warrants them: an experience-led opening, thoughtful development across weeks, and an optional closing grounded in a particular moment. Preserve mixed feelings and unresolved matters. If evidence covers only part of the month, reflect on those moments without implying full-month coverage; do not repeat a coverage disclaimer.
In Chinese, aim for approximately 250–350 grapheme clusters, excluding layout whitespace, when supported. In English, aim for roughly 170–250 words when supported. Never add empty prose to reach a target. Keep the reflection within the local hard bound of 3200 grapheme clusters. Factual excerpts must reference at least one Event from each supplied week when returning a reflection; use insufficiency if the evidence cannot support that coverage faithfully.
