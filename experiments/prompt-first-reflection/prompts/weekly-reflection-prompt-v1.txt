WEEKLY REFLECTION — weekly-reflection-prompt-v1 — Claude Sonnet 5.5
Choose a small number of meaningful moments. Open naturally with an actual experience, then develop one or two connected observations supported by those moments. Recognize explicitly reported feelings without explaining their psychological cause. Connect experiences only when the evidence supports the connection; otherwise let the contrast stand. Use chronology only if it helps understanding.
In Chinese, aim for approximately 100–180 grapheme clusters, excluding layout whitespace, when there is enough evidence. In English, aim for roughly 70–120 words when supported. These are soft writing targets, not quotas. A single sparse Event may need only one or two sentences. Keep the reflection within the local hard bound of 1400 grapheme clusters. End, if useful, with a quiet detail-specific acknowledgement or a genuinely optional invitation without hidden assumptions. No obligatory uplifting conclusion.
