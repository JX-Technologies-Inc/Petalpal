# Reviewer Guide

This is an independent blind review. Do not discuss research rows with the other reviewer. Review every row × label.

For each label, first rate applicability: NO (definition does not apply), PLAUSIBLE (may apply but alternatives remain), or CLEAR (clearly applies). Lock Stage 1 before starting Stage 2.

Then rate writer-owned evidence: NONE (no support for the writer’s own emotion), WEAK (indirect/ambiguous/speculative), or SUFFICIENT (enough textual support). Lock Stage 2 before eligibility.

Eligibility is mechanical: CLEAR and SUFFICIENT. Rank only eligible labels by text support strength; preserve ties and record tie groups. Do not hand-edit top-2. Leave any uncertain rating explicit; never infer from other rows.
