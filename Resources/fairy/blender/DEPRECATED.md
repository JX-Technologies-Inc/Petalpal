# Deprecated Fairy Blender experiments

Fairy V1 and V2 are deprecated as of the approved 2D-first pipeline decision on 2026-09-30. Do not use these `.blend` files, their renders or builder scripts as visual references for future Fairy production. Do not continue their models.

They remain on disk as historical experiments only. Current authority: `Resources/Icon/fairy structure.png`. Follow [`../production/README.md`](../production/README.md) for the approved painterly design and production preparation.
